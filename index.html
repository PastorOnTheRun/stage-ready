    Announcement leadership

    Always begin here before the details.

    Point students to what's happening this week and in the life of the church.

    1. 1Introduce yourselfTell the room your name before sharing any details.
    2. 2Explain the whySay what we are doing and briefly explain why it matters.
    3. 3Share the visionConnect at least one announcement to our vision: Believe in Jesus, Belong in community, and live Beyond ourselves.
